My Story

Real life.
Real wellness.

I'm Briana — a mom of six, a wife, a teacher, a trainer, and someone who knows firsthand what it feels like to be last on your own list.

I've spent ten years in the fitness industry — working in gyms, training clients one on one, and building a presence in the wellness community. And through all of it, I kept coming back to one truth: sustainable wellness isn't about doing more. It's about coming back to the well. Returning to what gives you life.

The Wellness Collective Studios was born from that truth. It's for anyone who is busy, stretched thin, and still showing up every day — and who wants to feel strong, healthy, and genuinely good in their body. Whether you're just starting out or you've been at it for years, whether you're coming alone or bringing your partner, you deserve a plan, a coach, and a community that actually gets it.

Whole body wellness. For life. Not doing more — doing good. That's what we're building here.

We can't wait to meet you!

    What We Offer

    Class Descriptions

    All levels and abilities welcome — unless otherwise noted.

    Yoga – Vinyasa Flow

    The Loft

    Move, breathe, and reconnect. Vinyasa Flow links breath with intentional movement through a fluid sequence designed to build strength, improve mobility, and create space in both body and mind. Expect a thoughtful balance of challenge and restoration, with options to make the practice your own. All levels are welcome.

    Sculpt Classic

    The Loft

    This Pilates-inspired mat class will challenge your muscular endurance, building strength by maximizing your movement. Using time, tension, pulses, holds, and high energy music, you'll feel strengthened throughout your entire body.

    Sculpt Strong

    The Well

    Grab your weights and get ready to work. Sculpt Strong combines focused strength training with targeted sculpting exercises to challenge your entire body. Expect defined sets, purposeful repetitions, and a mix of heavier strength work and smaller movements that bring the burn.

    Power Moves

    The Well

    Turn up the music and bring the energy. Power Moves is an upbeat, strength-focused class built around powerful, functional movement. Expect dumbbells, dynamic exercises, and athletic combinations designed to help you feel stronger and more capable — without turning the class into an all-out HIIT session. You'll work hard, move well, and leave feeling accomplished.

    Women, Weights & Worship

    The Loft
    Women Only

    A space to strengthen your body, quiet your mind, and fill your spirit. Women, Weights & Worship combines approachable strength training with Christian music, intentional movement, and moments of reflection. Each class begins and ends with purpose, creating space to set down what you're carrying and reconnect with what matters. Come exactly as you are — no fitness level or faith journey required to have it all figured out.

    Mom & Me

    The Loft

    A class for moms and children under 2! Bring your babe, move your body. The postpartum season is better with community and movement. Spots are limited to 6 — sign up early.

    Stretch & Flow

    The Loft

    Designed to leave you feeling limber, relaxed, and strong, this class takes you through gentle stretching and mobility movements, and integrates functional core work to improve your overall well-being!

    Small Group Training

    Valhalla Lifting Club

    Available by request! Sessions are held at Valhalla Lifting Club. Small group training is max 4 people, built around customized programming. Make your own group or request to be added to one — if you're ready to level up your strength training, this is for you!

    What is Restore?

    You give so much of yourself.
    This weekend is for you.

    Maybe you can't remember the last time you truly rested. Maybe you used to love to read, to sit by the water, to just be — and somewhere along the way, that version of you got buried under the weight of everything else.

    Restore is a weekend retreat designed to give that back to you. Nourishing food, intentional movement, lakeside rest, real community, and space to breathe. All in a beautiful setting, with women who get it.
