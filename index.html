My Story

Real life.
Real wellness.

I'm Briana — a mom of six, a wife, a teacher, a trainer, and someone who knows firsthand what it feels like to be last on your own list.

I've spent ten years in the fitness industry — working in gyms, training clients one on one, and building a presence in the wellness community. And through all of it, I kept coming back to one truth: sustainable wellness isn't about doing more. It's about coming back to the well. Returning to what gives you life.

The Well Studio was born from that truth. It's for anyone who is busy, stretched thin, and still showing up every day — and who wants to feel strong, healthy, and genuinely good in their body. Whether you're just starting out or you've been at it for years, whether you're coming alone or bringing your partner, you deserve a plan, a coach, and a community that actually gets it.

Whole body wellness. For life. Not doing more — doing good. That's what we're building here.

I'll meet you at the Well. — Briana

    What is Restore?

    You give so much of yourself.
    This weekend is for you.
